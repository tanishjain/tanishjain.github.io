References & methodology

Sources

Every claim in this archive is sourced or marked as unverified. This page lists all sources used, grouped by type, and explains the methodological decisions behind classification, dating, and attribution.

Methodology

How records are classified

Photographs versus other media

Media are classified as photograph, treated image, illustration, document, or unassessed. Programmes and newspaper scans are archival documents, distinct from photographs of the idol. A scene photograph shows a physical installation or setting; its date and provenance remain separate questions. Cutouts and devotional treatments can introduce elements absent from the original. Older card scans whose production process is uncertain are labelled photo-derived prints / original unverified and excluded from the photograph filter.

Year attribution versus independent confirmation

A source's year label is preserved as an attribution, distinct from the date an image was uploaded or published. Contemporary press galleries offer stronger year evidence than retrospective filenames. Known conflicts remain explicit. A dated publication does not by itself authenticate an original negative, identify its owner, or grant reproduction rights.

Year labels on each image — Contemporary report, Attributed year, Date uncertain, Date disputed, or Undated — express the nature of the source evidence available, not an independently authenticated capture date.

Rights and reproduction

Ganeshas of Mumbai is an independent archive based in the United States. Each displayed image has a credit and reuse information. Some records link to source material while reproduction is under review. Contact the archive with a contribution, correction, or question.

Missing years (annual-calendar collections)

Years with no located visual material are retained in the catalogue as ledger entries. Absence from this archive does not imply no documentation exists; it means none has been located in the course of current research. For temple and located-records collections, only actually documented years are displayed.

Dating and classification

Dating evidence and media classification are kept separate. A dated source label is not independent authentication; a contemporary publication offers stronger evidence than a retrospective one. Each record displays both the evidence and its limits.

Source corrections

A correction should retain its evidence: a dated source, original print, identifiable photographer or institutional record. The downloadable research notes preserve the unresolved questions and the next sources to seek.
