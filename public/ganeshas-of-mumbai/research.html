Research roadmap

Phases of research

Phase 1 — Foundations (complete)

All twenty collections have an initial research entry: institutional identity, available history sources, documented milestones, and located visual material where it exists. Dates and claims are scoped to their evidence. Photographs are only included after visual inspection; undated images are presented separately from dated records.

Phase 2 — Expanded visual chronologies

Annotated year-by-year records draw on surviving photographs, newspaper archives and documentary material. Unresolved year labels retain their conflicting evidence. Collections with a deeper research pass are marked Expanded research; the ledger records their actual coverage and remaining questions.

Phase 3 — Original scans, oral histories, permissions

Engaging with families and institutions who hold original material. Seeking permission to digitize and publish historic photographs. Recording oral histories from participants and their descendants with their consent.
